College History & Heritage

Tracing the remarkable journey of engineering education at President Ramon Magsaysay State University across the decades.

2002 - 2004

Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering (BSCE)

The BSCE program was first conceptualized under the Self-Liquidating Program Scheme in 2002 under University President Feliciano S. Rosete. Approved by the Board of Regents in 2003, it launched at the Iba Campus in 2003-2004 with close leadership from Engr. Liza M. Atienza and Prof. Cecilia A. Santiago. Its first batch graduated in March 2008 with a notable 70% passing rate in the PRC Licensure Examination.

EXPANSION ERA

Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Programs

Building on the industrial roots of Zambales, the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering curricula were founded to supply world-class technical talent for regional power plants, fabrication facilities, and international engineering enterprises.

TECHNOLOGY FRONTIER

Computer Engineering (BSCpE) & Mining Engineering

With the rise of the digital economy and mining resource development in Zambales, PRMSU inaugurated the Computer Engineering and Mining Engineering departments, equipping students with state-of-the-art computer labs, embedded design kits, and geological survey field training.
