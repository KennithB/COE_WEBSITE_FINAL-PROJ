COLLEGE DEAN

GILBERT D. DEBOMA, REE, M.Eng.

Engr. Gilbert D. Deboma has served the College of Engineering for 24 years with an Academic Rank of Associate Professor V. He holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from TIP Quezon City, an M.S. in Electronics Engineering from Andong National University (South Korea), and is pursuing his Ph.D. in Agricultural Engineering at Central Luzon State University. An active member of IIEE, he leads the college as College Dean and concurrent EE Department Chairperson.
